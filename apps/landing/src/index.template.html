A FEW THINGS, BEFORE YOU GO

Good questions.

Still have one? Let's talk ↗

Is ShowPilot just for churches?+

ShowPilot is built for churches and live event teams. Use it to coordinate a weekly service, a conference, or a production with a shared rundown and crew.

Do our volunteers need accounts?+

Crew can check in by scanning a QR code, without an account or an app install. Operators sign in to plan and run the show.

Can we keep the tools we already use?+

Yes. Connect tools like ProPresenter, OnTime, OBS, ATEM, and X32 or Wing. ShowPilot also has its own rundowns, timers, crew check-in, and chat.

What do we need to install?+

You can use ShowPilot in a browser on a laptop or tablet. To connect to equipment on your venue network, run ShowPilot Bridge on a venue computer. ShowPilot Desktop also supports local device control.

How does the founding member rate work?+
