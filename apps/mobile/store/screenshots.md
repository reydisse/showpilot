# Store screenshot plan

Capture screenshots from a signed production-candidate build using a dedicated demo workspace. Do not use real customer, volunteer, device, or venue data.

## Apple required sets

- iPhone 6.9-inch portrait: 1–10 screenshots. Use an accepted current size such as 1320 × 2868.
- iPad 13-inch portrait: 1–10 screenshots because `supportsTablet` is enabled. Use 2064 × 2752.
- Capture matching landscape sets only if the submitted app supports a useful landscape experience.

## Google Play required assets

- Phone screenshots: at least 2; provide 6 portrait screenshots at 1080 × 1920 or 1440 × 2560.
- Feature graphic: 1024 × 500, JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha.
- App icon: the verified opaque 1024 × 1024 source is `assets/showpilot-app-icon.png`; Play Console derives its delivery sizes.

## Six-shot story

1. Shows — upcoming productions with dates and clear status.
2. Live rundown — active item, synchronized timer, and operator controls.
3. Operations — current incidents and fast response actions.
4. Schedule — assignments, roles, service date, and custom call time.
5. Inbox and chat — assignment alert or mention plus persistent team chat.
6. Profile and settings — warm light/dark appearance and native notification controls.

## Capture rules

- Use the same fictional workspace and service across every frame.
- Keep the status bar clean and use current device framing only in marketing artwork, not uploaded screenshots.
- Show readable, truthful product UI. Do not add controls or capabilities in post-production.
- Verify both light and dark contrast, but keep each store set visually consistent.
- Remove debug menus, local URLs, seed credentials, personal names, real email addresses, and device serial numbers.
- Add concise overlay copy only after the raw screenshots are archived.
